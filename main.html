✦ Smart Travelling

Insider Travel Tips

Make every trip smoother, safer and more memorable

01

Book in Advance

Flights and hotels are cheapest 3–6 weeks before travel. Set price alerts and book early to save up to 40%.

02

Pack Light, Pack Smart

Use a carry-on only when possible. Roll clothes, use packing cubes, and always keep essentials in your day bag.

03

Get a Local SIM

Avoid roaming charges. A local prepaid SIM gives you maps, translation apps, and cheap calls throughout your trip.

04

Keep Digital Copies

Scan your passport, tickets, and insurance. Save them to cloud storage so you always have access even if bags are lost.

05

Eat Where Locals Eat

The best food is never in tourist zones. Follow the crowd, visit morning markets, and try roadside dhabas.

06

Stay Insured

Travel insurance is the best ₹500 you'll spend. It covers medical emergencies, cancellations, and lost luggage.
